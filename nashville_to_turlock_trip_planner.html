National Parks & Pass Math (Updated Without Sedona!)

Removing Sedona changes the National Park Pass math! See below how Option A (With Joshua Tree) compares to Option B (Without Joshua Tree) for 1 person:

🎟️ Official NPS $80 Pass Info ↗
🌵 If You Choose Option A (With Joshua Tree):
Buy the $80 America the Beautiful Pass (Saves $25!)

Individual entry fees for Carlsbad Caverns ($15) + Guadalupe Mts ($10) + White Sands ($25) + Saguaro ($25) + Joshua Tree ($30) = $105.00. Buying the $80 Annual Pass at Carlsbad Caverns saves you $25.00!

🌊 If You Choose Option B (Skip Joshua Tree):
Pay Per Park ($75 Total) OR Buy Pass for $80 (+$5)

Without Sedona and Joshua Tree, your remaining 4 paid parks (Carlsbad $15 + Guadalupe $10 + White Sands $25 + Saguaro $25) total $75.00—making individual entry $5 cheaper than the $80 pass! (Or pay $5 more for the $80 pass to get free entry to Yosemite & Sequoia near Turlock for the next 12 months!).
